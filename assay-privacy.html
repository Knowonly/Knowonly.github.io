Assay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Assay (“we”, “our”) is a private jewelry desk app for preliminary identification and reference valuation. This policy explains how information is handled.

What the app handles

On your device. Submissions you create (category, notes, photographs, optional short video, status, and any issued opinion) are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple’s standard on-device storage. Assay does not require a user account to use the app.

Paid download. The App Store one-time purchase is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Support and certificate email. If you email us (for support or to request a laboratory certificate process), we receive the address and content you choose to send, including any reference code you include.

Photo library access. The app requests access only so you can attach photographs or video from your library to a submission. Media is not published to a public feed.

What we do not do

  • No third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs
  • No sale of personal data to advertisers
  • No in-app marketplace, consignment, or jewelry checkout
  • No App Store in-app purchase for laboratory fees (those are settled offline if you choose a mail-in certificate)

Laboratory mail-in

If you request a formal laboratory certificate, shipping and lab processing happen outside the App Store. Additional personal and shipping details you provide by email or other offline channels are used only to arrange that engagement.

Children

Assay is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Delete individual submissions inside the app where available, or delete the app to remove on-device data
  • Refuse photo library permission (you will not be able to attach media)
  • Contact us to ask about support email retention
