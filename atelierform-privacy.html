Atelier Form — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Atelier Form (“we”, “our”) is a jewelry design education app. This policy explains how information is handled.

What the app handles

On your device. Lesson completion, last opened lesson, and optional drill notes you type are stored locally using Apple’s standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data does not leave the device. Atelier Form does not require a user account.

Paid download. The App Store one-time purchase is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Support email. If you email us, we receive the address and content you choose to send.

What we do not do

  • No third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs
  • No sale of personal data to advertisers
  • No in-app jewelry retail, consignment, or manufacturing checkout
  • No auto-renewing subscription inside this app

Children

Atelier Form is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove on-device progress and drill notes
  • Contact us about support email retention
